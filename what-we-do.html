What we do

Automate the workflow. Keep the people.

We integrate the technology stack you already own with modern AI, so the essential workflows toward new revenue run themselves. Your people get their hours back for the part a machine cannot do: the relationships.

How the engagement works

It is a subscription, not a project with an end date bolted on. A recurring fee for ongoing coaching, consulting, and training. Think of it as AI workflow intelligence advisory: someone whose job is to know what these tools can actually do this quarter, and to keep your operation pointed at the ones that earn their keep.

  • Coaching for the owner and the revenue leaders. Where the hours are going and what to stop doing.
  • Consulting on the stack. What to integrate, what to retire, what to leave alone.
  • Training and enablement, and this one stands on its own. Available as a separate scope of work for any client, in any industry, whether or not we touch your systems. Some teams do not need a rebuild. They need the process taught.

Small and midsize companies on purpose, and here is why

Large organizations fragment across departments and business units that all have to share tooling. That forces a square-peg fit: the tool gets chosen for the org chart instead of the work. Revenue operations teams inside those companies usually cannot move fast enough, and often do not have the authority to change anything even when they know exactly what is wrong.

Small and midsize is where the work actually changes somebody's day. The person who decides is in the room. The change ships in weeks. You can see it in the pipeline.

You are never locked in

We operate disclosed. Any incentive, commission, kickback, or referral agreement tied to a vendor we recommend gets disclosed to you. Neither we nor our clients are locked to a single AI or technology vendor, so you stay able to move as the technology changes.

That comes from firsthand experience as a captive insurance agent, where the compensation plan rewarded selling the suboptimal product to hit plan rather than serving the client. The whole practice is built the other way on purpose.
