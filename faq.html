Frequently Asked Questions

What is Smith Revenue Strategy?

A holding company. It owns two software products built for construction: BidStrike, for commercial subcontractors deciding which private bids to chase, and SLED Radar, for contractors selling into schools, cities, counties, and state agencies.

Who owns BidStrike and SLED Radar?

Smith Revenue Strategy owns both. It's my holding company.

Where do I get help with a product?

On that product's own site. Each product has its own site, and that's where the work happens, support included.

Do you use AI to do our work?

Yes, and here's exactly how. I use AI tools for the organizing, drafting, and research work. That's what they're good at. But everything you get from me, I've read and I stand behind. The composition gets automated. The accountability doesn't. If a recommendation reaches you, it's because I thought it through, not because a model produced it and I forwarded it.
