Consulting and go-to-market

Two leaders who needed the relationship work to scale without losing the relationship.

Both wanted the same thing: a white-glove referral program run by people, not a blast. Clients are described by role only.

champion_referrals

A nonprofit executive director

Warm introductions were happening, and then evaporating. Nothing carried the context from the person who offered a name to the conversation that followed.

We built a champion referral program end to end: who gets asked, what they are asked for, and how the introduction is handed off with the endorsement attached. The CRM was rebuilt to hold that, so an endorsement is a field that can be filtered rather than a note nobody reads.

What changed: referrals became a tracked motion with named owners and dates instead of a memory. Meetings booked off those introductions are attributed back to the person who made them.

gtm_operations

A development firm principal

A strong network and no operating layer under it. The founder was the pipeline, which meant the pipeline stopped whenever he did.

We stood up the go-to-market operations: contact data enriched and verified, the CRM schema rebuilt to carry nomination and endorsement detail, sequences that go out under the firm's own identity rather than a relay, and outreach logged back to the record automatically.

What changed: outreach runs on a schedule the principal does not have to hold in his head, and every touch lands on the contact timeline where the next person can see it.
