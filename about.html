About

Rodney Smith

By day I unlock revenue, fixing the tools and connecting banks and credit unions to the infrastructure they need to serve their clients' and members' money movement needs. Smith Revenue Strategy is my holding company. It owns two construction software products, BidStrike and SLED Radar.

LinkedIn

I've worked inside sales organizations that ran like a well-oiled machine. Clear priorities, clean systems, aligned teams. Work moved forward, people had margin, and there was energy left at the end of the day.

I've also worked inside the opposite. Layered approvals, broken handoffs, constant friction. Everything felt harder than it should be. And what stood out most wasn't the business impact. It was what that strain did to people. You could feel it follow them home.

That contrast stuck with me. How a business runs on the inside doesn't just affect performance. It shapes the lives of the people inside it. Their stress, their time, their presence with family. It's all connected.

That's why this work matters to me. Remove the friction, simplify the workflow, and build systems that actually support the people using them. Not just so the business can grow, but so the people in it can breathe a little more when they walk through their front door at night.

Because better systems don't just build better companies. They build better lives.

I should say where that comes from. I'm a Christian, and Scripture is direct about it. God's heart is for the freedom of the captive. I don't think that's only about the big things. A person spending four hours on work a machine should have handled has none of it, and their family feels that before they do.

Freedom is the point. The systems are just how I get there. My faith and my family come first, and everything else is downstream of that.

In one of those enterprise years I did two jobs because the system couldn't do one. We had money. Jessica did not have a husband. My kids had a father, but a ghost of one.

That's the thread through all of it. It's why I fix tools by day, and it's why BidStrike and SLED Radar exist. Both are built for construction shops where the owner has worn twelve hats and bootstrapped for a living.

A few numbers from the corporate years.

  • Rebuilt hiring and onboarding SOPs across a global team and cut new-hire ramp time by about half.

How I use AI in my own work.

I use AI tools for the organizing, drafting, and research work. That's what they're good at. But everything you get from me, I've read and I stand behind. The composition gets automated. The accountability doesn't.
