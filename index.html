Home of BidStrike and SLED Radar.

Smith Revenue Strategy is a holding company. It owns two software products built for construction: BidStrike, for commercial subcontractors deciding which private bids to chase, and SLED Radar, for contractors selling into schools, cities, counties, and state agencies. Each product has its own site, and that's where the work happens.

The products

Two products. Both clear the path to the next award.

They are not two versions of the same tool. Most shops need one of them, not both.

The invitations already arrive. There are more of them than anyone can read. Every bid invite lands on one board, scored on fit, general contractor quality, distance, and documents. The go or no-go call takes a minute instead of an afternoon. Fits when most of your revenue arrives as an invitation to bid.
