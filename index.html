Read the story behind that

It's rarely one bad tool. It's the drag between them.

01

Admin work eats selling time.

Nobody schedules the rekeying, the chasing, or the status update. It absorbs the hours between the calls that actually move revenue, and it never shows up on a report as time lost.

02

Tribal knowledge walks out the door with the person who has it.

When the way your business runs lives only in somebody's head, that person is the container. The tools that wear them down are what make them leave, and your operating knowledge leaves in the same box. Put it in the system instead and the next hire picks it up on day one, while the people who carried it get their minds back for the relationships only they can build.

03

Disconnected tools lose context at every handoff.

Every handoff is a place where somebody retypes what the last system already knew. The detail that gets dropped is usually the one the next person needed.
