Quarterly cohort

A room of operators getting real leverage out of AI.

Most operators are running AI the way it came out of the box. This is a small group that meets quarterly to close the gap between that and a system that actually carries weight in the business.

Ask for a seat at an info session

What happens in the room

  • Clarity on what you need AI to accomplish. Not a tour of tools. The two or three places in your business where it would change the week.
  • Focused priorities for what comes next. You leave with an order of operations, not a reading list.
  • Coaching through live issues. The other operators in the room have already hit your problem, or are about to.
  • A standing read on what actually changed this quarter. AI practice moves fast and most of the noise is not worth acting on. The room sorts that out together.

The rhythm

  • Three to four hours a quarter, 1:00 to 4:00pm.
  • Info sessions in October and December 2026. Come to one, see the format, meet the people.
  • The group starts in February 2027.

Pricing is covered at the info session, where you can see what you would actually be joining before anyone talks about money.

I run the room. Some of the coaching is delivered in collaboration with a partner practice, so the work lines up with engagements members already have underway rather than pulling against them.

How a seat works

Anyone can raise a hand. This is not invitation-only, and there is no list you have to already be on.

  • A two-way interview. You are deciding about the room as much as the room is deciding about you. Both halves of that matter.
  • Members approve new participants. Once the group is running, the people already in it have a say in who joins. That is what keeps the room worth sitting in.

The vetting is about fit for this particular room. It is not a verdict on whether AI can do something useful in your business.

If the room is not the fit right now

The Operator OS file structure is available either way. It is the context-rich setup the group works out of, and it stands on its own: your business written into plain files you own, so the tools have something real to work with.
